Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026  ·  Applies to chunkhost.xyz and panel.chunkhost.xyz

We collect as little data as possible and never sell it. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what your rights are. It's written in plain English on purpose.

1. Who We Are

ChunkHost is a Minecraft server hosting service operated as a sole proprietorship in Québec, Canada. We are the data controller for all personal information collected through our website and services.

If you have any privacy-related questions, contact us on Discord at discord.gg/axpmeFyUTE.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information that's necessary to provide the service:

Data Why we collect it How long we keep it
Email address Account creation, service communications Until account deletion
Username & display name Panel login identity Until account deletion
Payment information Billing (processed by Stripe — we never see full card details) Managed by Stripe per their policy
Server configuration & world data Running your Minecraft server Until service cancellation + 7 days

3. What We Don't Collect

  • We don't track you across other websites
  • We don't use advertising cookies or analytics tracking
  • We don't collect your full payment card number (Stripe handles this)
  • We don't read the contents of your Minecraft server or world files
  • We don't collect your real name unless you voluntarily provide it
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for:

  • Creating and managing your hosting account
  • Processing payments and sending invoices
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending service-related emails (downtime notices, billing reminders)
  • Preventing abuse and enforcing our Terms of Service
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly opt in.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your server data is stored in Québec, Canada on our hosting infrastructure. Backups are encrypted and stored with Backblaze B2.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data including:

  • HTTPS encryption on all web traffic
  • SSH key-only server access (no password login)
  • Firewall rules limiting access to necessary ports only
  • Fail2Ban protection against brute force attacks
  • Isolated Docker containers preventing cross-customer access

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by Canadian law.

7. Your Rights (PIPEDA & Quebec Law 25)

As a Canadian-operated service, we comply with PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) and Quebec's Law 25. You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of any optional data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us on Discord. We will respond within 30 days.

8. EU Customers (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing your data is contract performance (providing the hosting service you purchased). You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Cookies

Our main website (chunkhost.xyz) does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The Pterodactyl panel (panel.chunkhost.xyz) uses a session cookie strictly necessary for keeping you logged in. No third-party tracking cookies are present on any ChunkHost page.

10. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Users aged 13–17 may use the service with parental consent as outlined in our Terms of Service.

11. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Active account data — retained while your account is active
  • Server/world data — deleted 7 days after service cancellation
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years as required by Canadian tax law
  • Connection logs — deleted after 30 days
  • Support conversations — retained for 1 year then deleted

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email and our Discord server at least 14 days before they take effect. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

13. Contact Us
